title: The Case for Juries
description: >-
  How is the judge-centered legal system working in India today? The detention of Umar
  Khalid, an activist who protested the 2019 Citizenship Amendment Act, has gone on for
  more than 5 years, an experience consistent with the tremendous backlog of the system.
  What would happen if he had to have his case heard before a jury? This thought
  experiment of the jury trial in India is paired with a discussion of the legal system
  in Argentina where jury trials have recently been introduced in the most serious cases.
